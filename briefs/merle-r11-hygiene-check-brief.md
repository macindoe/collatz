### Half 2 — the four negative measurements

1. **The golden ratio.** `φ` is the worst-approximable irrational and its convergent denominators are exactly the Fibonacci numbers; had `log₂3` been of that type the cycle problem would plausibly be easy. It is not: 2000 partial quotients fit Gauss–Kuzmin with **chi-squared 0.00103**, denominator ratios run **1.02 to 55.6** against `φ`'s constant 1.618. His own scoping, which he says he first put too strongly: this closes arguments needing `log₂3` to be *statistically* peculiar; it does **not** close arguments resting on **effective diophantine input**, since an effective irrationality exponent is perfectly compatible with statistical ordinariness — as our own use of Rhin 1987 shows.
2. **The rhythm of the peaks.** No memory, no clustering (variance/mean² **0.831** against controls 0.745–0.949), no spectral line (top peak **5.62×** variance against a control maximum of **6.64×**). Includes his own withdrawn false alarm: a lag-1 autocorrelation of **−0.077** raised against an i.i.d. Gauss–Kuzmin null, which is the wrong null (the Gauss map makes consecutive partial quotients weakly dependent by construction). Recomputed against real continued fractions on a common footing, 1500 terms each: **`log₂3` −0.070, π −0.063, `log₂5` −0.104, `log₂7` −0.103**.
3. **The neighbours.** Across `ax+1` for odd `a = 3..31`: correlation between cycle count and approximation quality of `log₂a` is **+0.22** (Markov constant) and **−0.29** (largest partial quotient) — nothing — while **drift gives −0.57**. Worst-protected neighbour `a = 17` (partial quotient 215) has no cycles; best-protected in range `a = 21` has none either. The structural claim: **among odd `a ≥ 3`, `a = 3` is the only one with negative drift, since `log₂a < 2` forces `a < 4`** — structural rather than empirical. Stated caveat: for large `a` the orbits leave his search window, so those zeros mean "not found here"; the drift statement does not depend on the search.
4. **The drift from the inside.** Per step the north–south difference is `D(x) = log₂(3 + 1/x) − log₂(3 − 1/x)`, asymptotically `2/(3x·ln2)`. **Summed around a cycle that is exactly `n·δ`** — the same `δ` that makes T1's window finite; so the drift is not a new object but the seam identity in different clothes, "a third face of the same wall." One exact claim he has not seen stated anywhere: **the sign information equals the drift at exactly `x* = 7/3`**, since `(3 + 3/7)/(3 − 3/7) = 4/3` and `log₂(4/3) = 2 − log₂3` identically. Below `7/3` the sign term dominates; above it the terrain is grey. At `2^71` the ratio is `9.8·10⁻²²`.
