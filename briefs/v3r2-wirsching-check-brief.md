# Brief: does Wirsching's inverse-map 3-adic Markov process cover `13.6.3`(v) or `13.6.5`? (v3 round 2, final gate)

**Why this exists.** `briefs/v3r2-syrac-identity-findings.md` established that `aeh.md` `13.6.5`'s absorption law **is** Tao's `Syrac(Z_3)` in different coordinates (`y_3 = Syrac(Z_3)/2`), and closed with a pointer it could not follow: Tao's own footnote credits

> **G. Wirsching, _The Dynamical System Generated by the `3n+1` Function_, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 1681, Springer, 1998**

with *"the `3`-adic Markov process for the **inverse** Collatz map."* Our `y_3` is a **past-limit built from predecessor chains** — the inverse-map setting. Wirsching is 1998, twenty-one years before Tao.

**The repository already has a Wirsching verdict, and it may not cover this.** `publication.md` L21 records Wirsching as checked and dismissed: *"`3`-adic averages of stopping-time counting functions — a different `3`-adic use."* But that check was aimed at a **different target** — `itinerary.md` `14.15.3`(c)'s two-sided `Z_2 × Z_3` coding (see `itinerary.md` L126) — and at a different part of the monograph. It is not evidence about `13.6.3`(v) or `13.6.5`.

**This is a targeted attribution check, not a novelty re-sweep.** Two objects, named below. Do not survey the monograph, and do not re-open questions the repository has already settled against other targets.

## The two objects at stake

**Object 1 — `aeh.md` `13.6.3`(v), the joint product law under `B`** (L90–99). This is the one item the Tao check confirmed has *no* counterpart in Tao, and the literature check identified it as the paper's **entire remaining AEH content**. In substance: under the Bernoulli letter law, the conditional law of the door `y_n` given the past is exactly Haar-odd; hence `(m_n, q_n)` is independent of the whole past, `m_n` geometric(1/2), `q_n` Haar-odd given `m_n`; the absorption `a_{n+1}` is a function of the past alone; and the reconstructed window state has an exact product law. **The question: does Wirsching's inverse-map Markov process already contain this renewal/product structure, in any coordinates?**

**Object 2 — `aeh.md` `13.6.5`, the absorption law.** Already known to be Tao's (2019). **The question: does Wirsching's process predate Tao for this same object?** If so the attribution drafted for `13.6.5` names the wrong source, or needs both.

Secondary, only if the above are settled: whether Wirsching bears on `13.6.1` (letter law) or `13.6.2` (Bernoulli identification). `aeh.md` already frames those as classical and credits Lagarias/Terras/Everett, so the exposure there is low.

## The external work

LNM 1681 is a Springer monograph and may not be obtainable in full. Escalate in this order, and **say which rung each claim rests on**:

1. The monograph itself, if accessible.
2. Wirsching's own papers and survey articles — he restated much of this material in article form; a paper stating the inverse-map `3`-adic Markov process explicitly would settle the question as well as the book does.
3. Reviews with mathematical content (zbMATH, MathSciNet), the published table of contents and chapter abstracts.
4. Citing literature — Tao's footnote, Lagarias's annotated bibliographies, and any paper that describes what Wirsching's `3`-adic process *is*.

**A clean "could not establish from accessible sources" is an acceptable and useful outcome.** It is far better than a verdict inferred from a table of contents. If you reach that outcome, say precisely what would settle it and what access would be needed.
