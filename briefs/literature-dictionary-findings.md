### 1.2 The papers' conventions

- Paper 1 (`paper/collatz-reduced-v3.tex`, v3, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21730505): "Let `T(x) = (3x+1)/2^{v₂(3x+1)}` denote the odd-to-odd Collatz map on positive odd integers" is the first sentence of §1. The raw map carries no symbol ("the Collatz map", "the Collatz dynamics"); the one-division map is named in words twice in §5 ("the one-division map `y ↦ y/2` or `(3y+1)/2`") with no symbol; `T_N` is the block horizon of the AEH statement; `T_j` are partial sums in Theorem 4.5's proof; Inselmann's `T` is quoted once as "`T`-time". No `Syr`, `Col`, `T_1`.
- Paper 2 (`paper/collatz-mirror-v1.tex`, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21303918): "Fix the odd-to-odd Collatz map `T(x) = (3x+1)/2^{v₂(3x+1)}`" in §1; `T_3(ω) = (4ω−1)/3^{v₃(4ω−1)}` is the mirror map on cores in the ladder dual; no raw-map symbol; no `Syr`, `Col`, `T_1`.
- Both papers therefore use this record's convention (`T` odd-to-odd), not the literature's; a reader arriving from the PDFs has seen the wiki's `T`. The dictionary's convention note says so.
