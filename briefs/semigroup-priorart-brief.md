## 1. The object, stated precisely (compare like with like)

For each letter `(m, r)` with `m, r ≥ 1`, `reverse.md` 14.14.4.1 gives the exit map restricted to that stratum as an exact affine map:

```text
g_{m,r}(y) = α_{m,r}·y + β_{m,r},     α_{m,r} = 3^m / 2^(m+r),     β_{m,r} = (3^m − 2^m) / 2^(m+r)
```

Let **`S`** be the semigroup generated by `{g_{m,r} : m, r ≥ 1}` under composition — a sub-semigroup of the affine ("`ax + b`") group over `Q`. Two coordinates, with different characters:

- **Multiplier.** `α : S → Q*_{>0}`, `α(W) = 3^{M(W)} / 2^{S(W)}`, is a semigroup **homomorphism**. Since `2` and `3` are multiplicatively independent, `α` recovers the totals `(M, S)` exactly — and *only* the totals. It is blind to letter order.
- **Offset.** `β` is a **1-cocycle** over `α` (`β(W·W') = α(W')·β(W) + β(W')`, order convention to be fixed and stated explicitly in your findings — get this right, it is easy to get backwards). `β` carries all the ordering information, and is, up to normalisation, `cycles.md` 12.6.1's numerator `R_0`.
- **Fixed point.** `y* = β/(1 − α)`, matching `itinerary.md` 14.15.9's `y* = B_P/(1 − A_P)`; with `1 − α = q/2^S` and `q = 2^S − 3^M`, this is the fixed-point frame's `y* = a/q`, and `q = 1` (integer origin) is exactly the cycle condition.

Everything above is already proved in the wiki; it is restated here only so that your comparison against the literature is exact. **Do not re-derive it and do not re-verify it** — cite it.

## 2. The gate question

**Is `S` (or a recognisable variant of it) an object already named and studied in the 3x+1 literature or in the general theory of affine semigroups?**

Three outcomes, all acceptable, all reportable:

- **(A) Already studied.** Identify the object, its standard name, what is proved about it, and what the literature's obstruction is. This closes the framing as "known" and converts it into a citation obligation.
- **(B) Related but distinct.** Identify precisely how it differs (generators, ambient group, quotient/sub-object relationship), and whether the difference is the kind that matters.
- **(C) Not present.** Record what was searched and how, so the negative is auditable rather than an absence of effort.

## 4. The evaluation axis — this is the deliverable the author asked for

For **each** prior-art item that turns out to be genuinely related, answer these five questions in order, flatly:

1. **What object did they form?** Generators, ambient structure, and whether it is multiplicative (living in `Q*`) or affine (living in `Aff(1, Q)`).
2. **What is its relationship to `S`?** Same object / quotient / sub-object / image under a homomorphism / unrelated. Be exact; "similar in spirit" is not an answer.
3. **What did they prove?** State the actual theorems, not the abstracts' framing.
4. **What stopped them?** The stated obstruction, in their own terms where they state one. If they do not state one, say that rather than inventing one.
5. **Would our scaffolding have changed the outcome?** The candidates are: the exact local branching law (`reverse.md` 14.2.4), the stratum alphabet and the seam (14.14), the cylinder theorem (`itinerary.md` 14.15.1.5), and the exact whole-period height laws (14.15.9). **A verdict of "no — our scaffolding would not have helped" is a perfectly good finding and must be reported as readily as a yes.** Do not manufacture leverage.

## 5. One structural hypothesis to test — do not assume it

The main session's prediction, offered so you can test it rather than rediscover it, and **explicitly not a conclusion**:

> If the literature's 3x+1 semigroup is *multiplicative* (a sub-semigroup of `Q*`) while ours is *affine*, then theirs may be the image of ours under the multiplier homomorphism `α` — i.e. the abelianization, which forgets exactly the letter-order information in which the cycle question lives (`β`, hence `R_0`, hence `q | R_0`).

If that is right, it is a clean and useful finding: the known object is our `α`-coordinate, and the entire open content sits in the `β`-coordinate the known object discards. If it is wrong, say so plainly and show why. **Do not force this analogy** — a forced identification is worse than no identification, and this repository has a standing record of recording dead ends rather than dressing them up (`reverse.md` 14.4, 14.13 are the precedents).
