<p><strong>Abstract.</strong> We study the Collatz dynamics in a reduced coordinate system that compresses each deterministic valuation run into a single block, producing a self-map <i>F</i> on states (ω, <i>d</i>) — an odd core prime to 3 and a depth; the states appear independently as the skeleton rows of Williams (2026) — whose convergence problem and cycle set are exactly those of the Collatz map. In these coordinates the local arithmetic admits exact laws, all governed by one 2-adic quantity, the <i>anchor</i> <i>M</i>(ω) = −2 log ω / log 9 ∈ ℤ<sub>2</sub>: the exit valuation obeys the global law <i>s</i> = 2 + <i>v</i><sub>2</sub>(<i>d</i> − <i>M</i>(ω)) on the lifting classes and is constant on the rest; the depth evolution closes exactly in the anchor displacement together with a stated 3-adic absorption law; and the anchor increment obeys an exact law modulo any power of 2, computable from graded residues of the state. A finite window of digits and stratum labels consequently decides each step in an error-free trichotomy, while a digit-budget accounting indicates that no bounded window can decide infinite horizons — localizing the difficulty in the digit supply of the anchors. On the cycle side, a one-line elimination identity (the Böhm–Sontacchi cycle equation in block form) yields short rederivations of the classical exclusions at one, two and three blocks and of the classical trim uniform in the number of blocks <i>p</i> (Rozier 1991; Simons–de Weger 2005), whose constant degrades like (log<sub>2</sub> 3)<sup>−<i>p</i></sup>. We prove that this exponential loss is intrinsic to size arguments. An explicit family of configurations (<i>staircases</i>: divergent-orbit profiles bent into loops) meets every rotation's exact size condition with slack between two absolute constants at every period (<i>p</i> ≥ 16 by proof, 3 ≤ <i>p</i> ≤ 15 by finite check, <i>p</i> ∈ {2, 4} by exhibition), and, with every cycle element at least a threshold <i>X</i><sub>0</sub>, at every period <i>p</i> ≥ <i>p</i><sub>1</sub>(<i>X</i><sub>0</sub>), with slack exceeding the trim's own exponent by at most an absolute constant. The family realizes Rozier's 1991 extremal, which Simons–de Weger expected in 2005; the analogue in the cycle-length parameter, and the conclusion that uniform cycle exclusion needs divisibility input, are Halbeisen–Hungerbühler's (1997). Finally we state the equidistribution hypothesis implicit in the classical heuristics as a precise conjecture — that the block letters of uniformly sampled large starts carry, at every finite block length, the frequencies of an exactly computable Bernoulli law — prove its consequences conditionally, exhibit an unconditional base case inside the digit budget, and report a calibration campaign whose four apparent anomalies all dissolved under controls.</p>
