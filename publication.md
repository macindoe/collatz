3. **Classical foundations** (must be cited as the bedrock): Terras (1976) — parity-vector equidistribution, the ancestor of every "fair-coin" statement including AEH; Everett; Lagarias's surveys and annotated bibliographies; the 2-adic conjugacy of the Collatz map to the shift (Lagarias; Bernstein–Lagarias) — the deep reason bulk uniformity "wants" to be true. 4. **M. Inselmann, [arXiv:2402.03276](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.03276) (v3, Aug 2024) — the nearest neighbor of §13's consequences, and the one that subsumes them.** Unconditional, natural density 1: the two-sided trajectory envelope and the descent corollary described in the AEH verdict below, plus the parity first moment (Thm 1.6). Source read page by page, 2026-08-02: `briefs/v3r3-inselmann-horizon-findings.md`. ## Provenance (novelty-relevant)

The framework's origin is documented and predates all three contemporary neighbors: a hand-drawn note of 13 March 2023 (`sources/Collatz_conjecture_part1.png`, original file timestamp preserved, transcribed in README) already contains the seed/odd-core concept, the block cascade, the base-9 structure, and the Stage-4 question. Independent origination of the *coordinate system* — the (ω,d) structure, seed, and block cascade — three years before arXiv:2603.11066, with a complete draft history (sources/drafts v000–v078) and verification records.
