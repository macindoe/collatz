HALF 2 / K -- the neighbours: the structural drift claim for a*x+1
==============================================================================
  Derivation, in our own coordinates.
  Let a be ODD and consider the odd-to-odd map T_a(x) = (a*x + 1) / 2^v,
  v = v_2(a*x+1) >= 1 (a*x+1 is even because a and x are odd).  One step:
      log2 T_a(x) - log2 x = log2(a + 1/x) - v .
  Averaging convention, stated explicitly because this is where such claims
  fail: (1) the 1/x term is dropped -- it is O(1/x) and contributes a
  convergent total along an orbit, so it changes no drift SIGN; (2) v is
  averaged under the standard 2-adic model P(v = k) = 2^-k on k >= 1, whose
  mean is sum k*2^-k = 2 exactly.  Both are per-step arithmetic means over
  odd steps -- NOT time averages along one orbit, and not weighted by size.
  [ok ] E[v] = 2 exactly under P(v=k) = 2^-k   2.000000000000000
  So the per-step drift is  D_a = log2(a) - 2, and
      D_a < 0  <=>  log2 a < 2  <=>  a < 4 .
  [ok ] among odd a in 3..31, exactly one has negative drift, and it is a = 3   [3]
  [ok ] the equivalence log2 a < 2 <=> a < 4 is exact (a integer: a <= 3)
  drift at a=3: -0.415037   a=5: +0.321928   a=7: +0.807355
