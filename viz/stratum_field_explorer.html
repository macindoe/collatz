Stratum field explorer

Every step of the reduced map G carries a letter (m, r); this is that alphabet as a plane, m across, r up. First, because it is the opposite of the anchor field: this plane is an alphabet, not a state space — each cell is one residue class mod 2m+r+1 holding infinitely many doors y (Lemma 14.15.1.3(i)), and no map carries cell to cell, since which letter comes next depends on the y you stand on, not on the cell. So every colour below except the letter law is a property of one chosen representative — the smallest live door (3∤y) on that stratum — named in the tooltip.
