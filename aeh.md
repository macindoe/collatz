# 13. The Anchor Equidistribution Hypothesis

The statistics door of the program (README; `11.8.7.7`). The digit-budget theorem shows bounded windows cannot decide infinite horizons; what typical-orbit control *needs* is a statistical hypothesis about the anchor digits. This section makes that hypothesis exact, derives its consequences as conditional theorems, and calibrates it empirically. Proof effort remains parked per the stopping rules: the underlying question — digit statistics of `2`-adic logarithms — is beyond current theory. The deliverable here is precision, not proof.

## 13.1. Two regimes, one lesson

Orbit statistics mix two different objects. The **bulk** — visits while `x` is large — behaves measure-like. The **bottom** — the fixed, finite drainage basin of small integers every convergent orbit funnels through — has wildly non-uniform statistics (e.g., `P(ω ≡ 1 mod 32 | class (1, 2))` is `0.499` there vs `0.25` uniform) because those are the digits of *specific small numbers*, not samples from a measure. Whole-orbit averages conflate the two: the apparent violations of fair-coin behavior found first in calibration (`13.4`, runs E4b/E6) dissolved almost entirely when the regimes were separated (E7). Any precise equidistribution statement must therefore be a *bulk* statement. The historic ledger (`11.8.4.4`) should be read the same way.

## 13.2. The hypothesis

Fix a depth `k` and a **depth cap** `D_k` — any finite cutoff, fixed once together with `k`. The cap does one job, keeping the window alphabet finite; by `13.6.3`(iii) the capped depth is an exact function of a bounded two-sided letter window whenever `D_k ≤ W`, with no exceptional event at all. The **depth-`k` window state** of `(ω, d)` is `(ω mod 2^(k+2), min(d, D_k))` together with validity data; by `11.8.7.6.1` it decides the next step's `s` and `3`-gain in an error-free trichotomy with undecided rate `~2^-(k+1)`.

Let `π_k` denote the **exact product law**: the `ω`-residues Haar-uniform among the valid (= odd) residues and independent of the depth, and the depth the exact convolution

```text
d = m + a,    m geometric(1/2)  (P(m = j) = 2^-j),    a the absorption,    m INDEPENDENT of a.
```

This law is *derived*, not posited — it is `13.6.3`(v) — and its depth marginal is computed in closed form at `13.6.5`, which is where the values live. The stationary law of the exact window chain is a different object: a `~1%`-accurate model of this marginal, internal to this record, whose exact discrepancy from it is recorded at `13.6.5`.

**Hypothesis 13.2.1 (AEH, ensemble form).** Fix a depth `k`, a horizon rate `θ > 0`, and a cut sequence `X_N → ∞` with `log X_N = o(log N)`. For each `N`, draw `x` uniformly from the odd integers of `[N, 2N)`, set `(ω_0, d_0) = R(x)` and `(ω_{n+1}, d_{n+1}) = F(ω_n, d_n)`, and let `ν_{k,N}(x)` be the unweighted empirical distribution of the depth-`k` window states over the **bulk visits** among the first `T = ⌈θ log₂ N⌉` — those with `x_exit > X_N` — each qualifying visit counted once, with no per-orbit reweighting (`13.5`). Then for every `ε > 0`, the density of starts `x ∈ [N, 2N)` with `‖ν_{k,N}(x) − π_k‖ > ε` tends to `0` as `N → ∞`, for every admissible `θ` and `(X_N)`.

**Why the ensemble, and what the cut is now for.** There is one limit, `N → ∞`; the sample grows because the sampling scale grows, not because any orbit is run forever. No single-orbit form is available: above a fixed cut a convergent orbit supplies finitely many qualifying visits and, once the cut exceeds its maximum, none, so a limit in orbit length is empty rather than delicate — in either order, and for a diagonal cut `X(n)` too. The bulk cut keeps a smaller job, excising the bottom regime of `13.1` for those sampled orbits that reach it inside the horizon; `log X_N = o(log N)` keeps it from becoming a selection rule, a binding cut being exactly the protocol that manufactured `13.4`'s drift artifact and `13.5`'s phantom cell. For `θ < 1/β`, `β = 2(2 − log₂3) = 0.8301…`, the cut binds on a vanishing density of starts, the tally denominator is the deterministic `⌈θ log₂ N⌉`, and `13.5`'s standing rule is satisfied as written. The sample space is starting *values*, not states: natural density on pairs `(ω,d)` is not canonical, and the integer form is the one with an exact base case below. Because the bad density vanishes at every scale, the union of the bad sets has natural density zero in the integers — so the statement does deliver "almost every integer", for a finite-horizon property, with the exceptional set depending on `ε` and `θ`.

**Base case, and where the content is.** By Theorem `14.15.1.5` (itinerary.md) the odd integers following a given length-`n` letter word form exactly one class mod `2^{S+1}`, `S = Σ(m_i + r_i)`; so for `x` uniform on `[2^L, 2^{L+1})` the length-`n` word is *exactly* `B`-distributed on `{S + 1 ≤ L}`, and `TV(Law(W_n), B^{⊗n}) ≤ P_B(S_n ≥ L)`. With `E[m + r] = 4` (`13.6.1`) this is exponentially small for `n ≤ (1/4 − ε)L`; concentration under `B` and the dictionary of `13.6.3`(iii)–(iv) carry it to the window states. **Hypothesis 13.2.1 is therefore a theorem for every `θ < 1/4`.** A full descent takes `1/β = 1.2047…` blocks per bit — `4.8` times as long — so the hypothesis is precisely the assertion that equidistribution survives past the digit budget of stage4.md `11.8.7.7`, whose `σ ≈ 4.0` is this `E[m + r] = 4`. The calibration record is measured well past that frontier: the flagship run (starts `[2^70, 2^71)`, burn-in `10`, horizon `30`) runs `2.29 ×` the budget, with `23` of its `30` tallied blocks beyond it. The `1/4` frontier is this argument's, and the classical cylinder count's, not a barrier: for the *trajectory envelope* and for the ledger's *first moment* it has been crossed unconditionally at natural density `1`, out to exactly `1/β` (Inselmann; `13.3.2`), by a different technique. What has not been carried past it, as far as the literature check reached, is the distributional content — the full `2^-j` marginal at every `j`, the `1/3` rate, the depth law, `π_k` as a measure — and that is where AEH's content lives.

Supporting exact facts (unconditional): under `π_k`, the marginal of `s` is exactly the ledger — `P(s = 1) = 1/2`, `P(s = 2) = 1/4`, `P(s = j) = 2^-j` — by the residue-class count (`11.8.1.3.1`) plus the geometric shell law on the lifting classes; `3`-gain has probability exactly `Σ_(j even) 2^-j = 1/3` (`9.3`); and `a_+ = 0` exactly when `s` is odd, so shallow blocks never absorb `3`s. The class process under `π_k` is an explicit finite Markov chain with computable entries (e.g., from class `(1 mod 8, d odd)` the next depth is exactly `1`; from `(5 mod 8, d odd)`, `P(d_+ even) = 2/3` exactly). A symbolic form of the hypothesis is recorded at itinerary.md `14.15.2`: AEH is precisely the statement that the stratum words of the bulk segments of uniformly sampled starting values (`13.2.1`) equidistribute against the itinerary cylinder measure.

## 13.3. Conditional consequences (theorems modulo AEH)

**13.3.1 (ledger).** AEH at depth `k` implies the frequency ledger with explicit error `O(2^-k)`: the one-step trichotomy converts window frequencies into `s`-frequencies exactly, except on the undecided set of `π_k`-measure `~2^-(k+1)`. In `13.2.1`'s form: for every `k`, every horizon rate and every `ε`, all but a vanishing density of starting values of a given size carry the ledger to within `O(2^-k) + ε` along their first `⌈θ log₂ N⌉` bulk blocks. Letting `k → ∞` is a diagonal step — valid along any `k(N) → ∞` slow enough that the `k`-dependent densities still vanish, not uniformly — and gives the ledger exactly, in the limit, along those segments. There is no statement here about any fixed orbit's infinite tail: the exceptional set is a density-zero set of *starting values* at a prescribed finite horizon.

**13.3.2 (3-gain rate; and why no drift consequence is claimed).** AEH implies the `3`-gain rate is exactly `1/3` (measured along orbits: `0.3352`, `1.1σ` from `1/3`): `s` even is window-decidable and has `π_k`-probability `Σ_(j even) 2^-j = 1/3`, and the conclusion has the same density-of-starts form as `13.3.1`. **No drift or contraction consequence is carried, for two independent reasons.** First, it does not follow: the per-block increment is `m_+·(log₂3 − 1) − s`, and `m_+`, `s` are unbounded; convergence of window-state frequencies at each fixed `k` gives `liminf` bounds on their empirical means (Fatou) but no `limsup` on `m_+`, and the drift needs the latter, i.e. a uniform-integrability input equidistribution does not supply. Second, it would be worth nothing if it did: the corresponding trajectory statement is an unconditional theorem at natural density `1`. Inselmann (*An Approximation of the Collatz Map and a Lower Bound for its Average Total Stopping Time*, arXiv:2402.03276, 2024) pins `(3/4)^k m^{1−ε} ≤ Syr^k(m) ≤ (3/4)^k m^{1+ε}` simultaneously for all `k ≤ (log₂(4/3))^{-1} log₂ m` for a set of odd `m` of natural density `1` — a two-sided envelope, at exactly the horizon `θ = 1/β` that `13.2.1` names as a full descent — and his Corollary `1.4` gives descent below `m^ε` there, extending Korec's `x^{0.7924}` (Math. Slovaca 44 (1994), 85–89) to every exponent and improving Tao's logarithmic-density theorem to natural density for these thresholds. His `α = (log 2)^{-1}` is `13.2.1`'s `θ = 1/4` and his `α = 2(log(4/3))^{-1}` is `θ = 1/β`; the classical frontier and its unconditional crossing are the same two numbers seen from the other side. The drift itself remains a *measurement* on this page, under `13.5`'s standing rule: `−0.8367 ± 0.0060` per block and `−0.4166 ± 0.0037` per odd step (`13.4`).

**13.3.3 (what AEH does not give).** Even in full, AEH yields *density* statements: for each scale, all but a vanishing density of starting values, over a bulk segment of prescribed finite length. It yields nothing about any individual orbit, nothing about any orbit's infinite tail, and it does not iterate — the image of a density-one set of starts need not be density-one at the next scale. That non-iteration is the classical obstruction, not an in-house caveat: Tao records it for his own theorem in the same words (the Collatz iteration may send almost all of `[1,x]` into a very sparse subset of `[1,x^θ]`), and nothing here removes it. The exceptional tails — sustained deviations of exactly the staircase profile (`12.8.4`) — have `B`-probability zero as infinite words and vanishing density as segments, yet are not excluded for any *individual* orbit. AEH is the precise form of the missing statistical half; it is not a route to full convergence. This matches the program's honest scope (`11.8.4.4`, README). The staircase is simultaneously the cycle-sharpness family; the one-configuration-both-halves synthesis is charted at bridge.md `16.4.6`.

## 13.4. Calibration record (2026-07-08)

Eight experiment rounds, `experiments/aeh_calibration.py`. Statistics use per-orbit means with across-orbit standard errors (orbits independent; within-orbit pooling would overstate significance). This and `13.5`'s standing rule are one rule, and the reconciliation is load-bearing now that `13.2.1` is a per-start (quenched) statement and therefore needs per-orbit statistics: **a per-orbit mean is safe exactly when its denominator is deterministic** — fixed horizon, no stopping rule, a cut that does not bind — and unsafe when the denominator is the random count of qualifying visits, which is the ratio estimator `13.5` forbids. One notational gap, recorded once: the statements cut on `x_exit > X` while the code cuts on the core, `ω_+ > X` (`aeh_calibration.py` L361/L402, `aeh_symbolic.py` L566). Since `x_exit + 1 = 2^(m_+) 3^(a_+) ω_+`, cutting on `ω_+` is the strictly stronger cut; in these runs neither binds, so no number below depends on the choice. Findings:

* **Bulk uniformity confirmed** (cut `ω > 2^24`, ~1,600 orbits/cell): digit conditionals flat — `P(bits 3–4 | class (1,2)) = 0.2533` vs `0.25` (`0.8σ`); `(7,1)` cell `0.5017` vs `0.5` (`0.4σ`); consecutive-pair cell `(s, s') = (4,3)` at `0.1277` vs independent prediction `0.128` (`-0.1σ`). The `s`-marginals, `d`-law, and class-transition structure all match the exact chain.

Methodological caution recorded: three successive apparent "discoveries" during calibration (an `s = 3` marginal excess, orbit pair correlations, digit biases) each dissolved under better controls (larger starts, per-orbit statistics, bulk/bottom separation). The controls are now standard for this page.
* **The exact chains work**: the `(class, d)`-chain reproduces the orbit `d`-law to `~1%` and the naive `2^-k` ledger emerges from pure counting — the ledger is a *theorem about* `π_k`, and the empirical question is only whether orbits follow `π_k`.
* **External replication (measured grade, 2026-07-18):** an independent implementation by Eric Merle (3,000 orbits, cuts 2^20 and 2^30) reproduces the class skeleton's two exact values and the `2^{−j}` ledger, and measures the 8-class transfer-matrix spectrum at `|λ₂| ≤ 0.06` (our re-run: `0.028/0.036` at the two cuts — `experiments/merle_aeh_key_check.py`); his flagged drift artifact (`−0.33/−0.36` vs `−0.415`) is confirmed to be survivorship bias from the cut protocol: under this page's fixed-horizon standing rule the drift is `−0.4166 ± 0.0037` per odd step (`log₂3 − 2 = −0.4150`) and `−0.8367 ± 0.0060` per block (`2(log₂3 − 2) = −0.8301`). Spectral numbers are measurements, not theorems; the generic-face scope of 13.3.3 is unchanged.

## 13.5. The `(1 mod 8, d = 1)` anomaly: resolved (protocol artifact), with an exact lemma as consolation

One cell initially refused to clear: bulk visits to class `(ω ≡ 1 mod 8, d = 1)` appeared to prefer `ω ≡ 25 (mod 32)` at `0.2677` vs `0.25` — `z = 5.0` over `2,610` independent orbits, surviving a `2^40` cut, unpredicted by the depth-5 chain. The follow-up (2026-07-08, `experiments/aeh_anomaly.py`) resolved it.

**The decisive test.** Fixed-horizon, unweighted sampling — uniform large starts, exactly `T` steps, no stopping rule, per-visit pooling — shows the cell is *flat*: `P(ω ≡ 25 mod 32 | class) = 0.2503 ± 0.0015` pooled over `84,739` visits, flat at every horizon `T = 1..32` and in every clean altitude band of a `150`-step run. The invariant law is uniform here. The anomaly lived in the measurement protocol, not the dynamics.

**Lemma 13.5.1 (deterministic routing from class `(1,1)`).** For `ω ≡ 1 (mod 8)`, `d = 1`, the residue `ω mod 32` exactly determines the next class: `1 → (1,1)` (immediate self-return), `9 → (7,1)`, `17 → (5,1)`, `25 → (3,1)` — the last being the lifting class. **Proof.** `s = 1` and `C = 3ω + 1`, so `v_2(C) = 2` and `ω_+ = (3ω+1)/4`, whose residue mod `24` is a fixed function of `ω mod 32`; `a_+ = 0` since `C ≡ 1 (mod 3)`. Verified exhaustively on `26,673` sampled states, zero exceptions. ∎

**The artifact mechanism.** The earlier estimator took per-orbit *ratios* over each orbit's qualifying visits, with early stopping. By Lemma `13.5.1`, residue `1` self-loops (creating runs of consecutive visits), while residue `25` routes into the deep-descent lifting class. Orbits drawing more `25`s therefore descend faster and accumulate *fewer* qualifying visits, so each `25` carries a larger per-orbit weight — a textbook ratio-estimator bias, aimed at exactly one residue of exactly one class by exact arithmetic. This also explains the cell-specificity: `(1,1)` is the unique class with a deterministic self-loop channel. The absorption tail of long runs (orbits reaching the fixed point and tallying `ω = 1` forever) produces the complementary distortion.

**Standing rule (added to the calibration methodology).** Statistics on this page use fixed-horizon, unweighted, per-visit sampling from uniform starts. Ratio estimators over correlated visit sequences are forbidden — this cell is the reason.

**Status: resolved.** This was the fourth and final apparent discovery of the calibration campaign, and the most instructive: the "anomaly" was the exact transition structure of the reduced map reflected back through a biased estimator. Bulk uniformity now stands **unqualified** at all tested depths and cells.

## 13.6. The genericity form of AEH: the Bernoulli identification and the named equivalence

This section **names** the hypothesis in symbolic coordinates; it does not move it. The pointer sentence at itinerary.md `14.15.2` — the cylinder measure is exactly what `π_k` quantifies along orbits — is here upgraded to a proved, named equivalence: AEH is the assertion that integer orbits are *generic points* of an explicitly identified Bernoulli system. The identification is the classical `2`-adic coding of the Collatz map (Terras 1976, Everett 1977; Lagarias 1985; Bernstein–Lagarias 1996 — pinned at itinerary.md `14.15.1`/`14.15.3`) read in the door/`(m,r)` alphabet fixed by the seam (reverse.md `14.14`); everything classical stays classical, and the proofs below run directly through the wiki's own cylinder results rather than through literature attributions. AEH's status is unchanged by anything in this section: hypothesis, calibrated, proof effort parked (README stopping rules); `13.3.3`'s scope governs — even in full, AEH yields density statements about starting values at a finite horizon, and nothing about an individual orbit.

Throughout: `G` is the door-coordinate reduced map (reverse.md `14.14.3`), total on all odd integers with every output a live door (`14.14.7.1`); `stratum(y) = (m,r)` with `m = v_2(y+1)`, `q = (y+1)/2^m`, `r = v_2(3^m q − 1)` (itinerary.md `14.15.1.1`); a **letter** is a stratum value, and the **letter word** of `y` is `(stratum(G^i(y)))_{i≥0}`. Haar measure on the odd `2`-adics is normalized to mass `1` ("Haar-odd").

**Lemma 13.6.1 (letter law).** Under Haar-odd, `P(stratum = (m,r)) = 2^{−(m+r)}` exactly, for every `m, r ≥ 1`; equivalently, the two letter components are **independent, each geometric(1/2)**: `P(m = j) = 2^{−j}`, `P(r = j) = 2^{−j}`. In particular `E[m] = 2`, `P(m odd) = 2/3`, `P(r even) = 1/3`.

**Proof.** By Lemma `14.15.1.3`(i) (itinerary.md), the `y` with `stratum(y) = (m,r)` form exactly one residue class mod `2^{m+r+1}`, which is odd; its Haar-odd mass is `2 · 2^{−(m+r+1)} = 2^{−(m+r)}`. The factorization `2^{−(m+r)} = 2^{−m} · 2^{−r}` with `Σ_m 2^{−m} = Σ_r 2^{−r} = 1` is the independence statement. ∎

**Theorem 13.6.2 (Bernoulli identification).** Let `X_sing := {odd y ∈ Z_2 : G^n(y) = −1 for some n ≥ 0}` — the points whose forward `G`-orbit hits the single point where `m = v_2(y+1)` is undefined (`v_2(0) = ∞`; the coding's one singular point, recorded at itinerary.md `14.15.3`(a)'s remark and `14.15.6`). Then:

1. `X_sing` is **countable**, hence Haar-null. (On each stratum class, `G` is the affine bijection `y_0 + 2^{m+r+1}t ↦ G(y_0) + 2·3^m t` of Lemma `14.15.1.3`(ii), a homeomorphism from the class onto all of odd `Z_2` since `3^m` is a `2`-adic unit; so `G^{−1}(−1)` has exactly one point per stratum — countably many — and `X_sing = {−1} ∪ ⋃_n G^{−n}(−1)` is a countable union of countable sets.)
2. Off `X_sing`, the coding `Φ: y ↦ (stratum(G^i(y)))_{i≥0}` is defined for all time, and it is a **bijection** from `(odd Z_2) ∖ X_sing` onto the **full shift** `{(m,r) : m,r ≥ 1}^N`: injective because two points following the same word agree mod `2^{S_n+1}` for every prefix (`S_n ≥ 2n → ∞`; the argument of Theorem `14.15.3.2`, verbatim for `Z_2` points); surjective because every right-infinite word `W` has the unique nested-cylinder point `y_2(W)` (Definition `14.15.3.1`), which follows `W` for all time — every iterate carries a stratum, so `y_2(W) ∉ X_sing`.
3. `Φ` intertwines `G` with the shift: `Φ ∘ G = σ ∘ Φ` on the domain (`G` is `Z_2`-continuous on each stratum class by the affine formula, and maps the class of a word to the class of its shift).
4. `Φ` pushes Haar-odd to the **Bernoulli product measure** `B := ⊗_{i≥0} (geom(1/2) × geom(1/2))`: by Theorem `14.15.1.5`, the followers of a length-`n` word `W` are one odd class mod `2^{S+1}` (`S = Σ_i (m_i+r_i)`), of Haar-odd mass `2^{−S} = Π_i 2^{−(m_i+r_i)}` — exactly `B`'s cylinder mass. Cylinders generate both σ-algebras, and `Φ` matches them class-for-class, so `Φ` is bimeasurable.

Consequently `(odd Z_2, Haar, G)` is **measure-isomorphic to the Bernoulli shift** on the alphabet `{(m,r)}` with weights `2^{−(m+r)}`, and — pulling `B`'s shift-invariance back through `Φ` — **Haar-odd is `G`-invariant**. ∎

**Content — framing (per the mandate).** This is not new dynamics: the raw-parity form of the same statement — the parity-vector coding conjugates `T` on `Z_2` to the shift, carrying Haar to the fair coin — is the classical result of the pinned sources above, and the door-alphabet form is that fact read through the seam, with `G = T^m` (the block-map identity, reverse.md `14.14.7.1`) regrouping the parity word into `(m,r)`-letters. The isomorphism is proved here in three steps from the wiki's own results (`14.15.1.5` + `14.15.3.1`'s nested cylinders + the mass count of Lemma `13.6.1`), and the classical literature is cited for context rather than for the lemma, per the register norm: no attribution of the door-alphabet Bernoulli form itself is claimed or needed.

**Verified** — `experiments/aeh_symbolic.py`, fresh code (imports nothing from `itinerary_coding.py`, `aeh_calibration.py`, or `aeh_anomaly.py`), 2026-07-23. Letter law: exhaustive over all 36 strata `m,r ≤ 6`, each exactly one odd class mod `2^{m+r+1}`, `0` failures; totality on all odd `y < 2^15`. Word law: exhaustive over all `81` two-letter words (letters `≤ 3`) and all `64` three-letter words (letters `≤ 2`), each exactly one odd class mod `2^{S+1}`, `0` failures. Pushforward and renewal (sampled, seed `31004`, `200,000` uniform odd `y < 2^40`): `G(y) mod 128` uniform over odd residues (max relative deviation `0.052`, `2.9σ` at the worst of 64 cells), `q mod 64` uniform, `m ⊥ q mod 8`, and `G(y) mod 64` uniform conditionally on `stratum(y) = (1,1)` — all within `5σ`, `0` structural failures.

**Lemma 13.6.3 (the dictionary: time alignment and the two-sided window reconstruction).** Fix an `F`-orbit of states `(ω_0,d_0), (ω_1,d_1), …` with exits `y_n := x_exit` of edge `n → n+1`, and let `(m_n, r_n) := stratum(y_n)` be its letter word. Write `a_{n+1} := v_3(y_n + 1)` (the edge's absorption: `y_n + 1 = C_n/2^{s_n}`, so `v_3(y_n+1) = v_3(C_n) = a_+` of edge `n`). Then:

**(i) Time alignment (the first hard point, engaged).** The letters, the `F`-blocks, and the `T`-steps are synchronized as follows, with nothing waved at. (a) *Letters per block:* the doors chain under `G` (`G(y_n) = y_{n+1}`, semiconjugacy `14.14.3`), `state(y_n) = (ω_{n+1}, d_{n+1})`, and letter `n` is exactly `(m_+` of edge `n`, `s_{n+1})` — the identifications of reverse.md `14.14.6` — so there is exactly **one letter per `F`-block**, with a fixed one-index offset between letter time and state time (irrelevant to every Cesàro limit below). **(ii) The future determines the `2`-adic component.** `q_n := (y_n+1)/2^{m_n} mod 2^{k+2}` is an explicit function of letters `n, …, n+⌈(k+1)/2⌉`: their cylinder class pins `y_n mod 2^{S+1}` with `S ≥ m_n + k + 2` (Theorem `14.15.1.5`).

**(iii) The past determines the `3`-adic component (the second hard point, engaged).** For every `W ≥ 1`, `y_n mod 3^W` is an explicit function of letters `n−W, …, n−1` **alone**: the synchronization corollary (reverse.md `14.14.8.3`) with `Σ m ≥ W` guaranteed by `m_i ≥ 1`. Hence, for `n ≥ W`: the absorption `a_{n+1} = v_3(y_n+1)` is determined exactly whenever `a_{n+1} < W`, and the exceptional event `{a_{n+1} ≥ W} = {y_n ≡ −1 (mod 3^W)}` is itself determined by the same `W` letters. The depth is `d_{n+1} = m_n + a_{n+1}` (the seam: `y+1 = 2^m 3^a Ω`, `D = m + a`, reverse.md `14.14.7`), so for any cap `D_k ≤ W` the **capped depth `min(d_{n+1}, D_k)` is determined by letters `n−W..n` with no exception at all** (`a ≥ W` forces `d > D_k`, so the cap applies); and the core residue `ω_{n+1} mod 2^{k+2} = q_n · 3^{−a_{n+1}} mod 2^{k+2}` is determined off the exceptional event (it needs `a mod 2^k`, i.e. `a` exactly). The window state of `13.2` at visit `n+1` — with its stratum labels `(s, σ, a_+)` per stage4.md `11.8.7.6`, the reading of `13.2`'s "validity data" — is therefore an explicit function of the bounded two-sided letter window `n−W, …, n+⌈(k+1)/2⌉`, exactly, off a past-determined exceptional event.

**(iv) The exceptional tail.** `P_B(a ≥ j) ≤ 2·(0.93)^j` for every `j ≥ 1`; the exact values are far smaller (`1/3, 2/63, ≈ 0.0061, …`; `13.6.5`). **Proof.** Under `B`, condition on the `i` letters nearest `y_n`; prepending one more letter `(m',r')` shifts `y_n`'s offset by `A_i β'` with `v_3(A_i) = M_i` exactly and `β' ≡ (−1)^{r'+1} (mod 3)` (`14.14.8.2`, and the mod-`3` evaluation `β' ≡ −2^{−r'}`), so conditionally the `3`-adic digit of `y_n` at level `M_i` takes two distinct values with probabilities `2/3` and `1/3`, and the digit is final (later prepends shift by multiples of `3^{M_{i+1}}`, `M` strictly increasing). Matching any prescribed digit therefore has conditional probability `≤ 2/3`, and `{a ≥ j}` pins the digit at every level `M_i < j`: `P(a ≥ j) ≤ E[(2/3)^{I(j)}]` with `I(j) = #{i : M_i < j}`. For any `t`, `P(I(j) < t) = P(M_t ≥ j) ≤ (1+√2)^t 2^{−j/2}` (Markov with `E[2^{m/2}] = 1+√2`), so `E[(2/3)^{I(j)}] ≤ (2/3)^t + (1+√2)^t 2^{−j/2}`; taking `t = ⌊j/5⌋` gives the bound. ∎

**(v) Renewal, and the product structure of the window-state law under `B`.** Under Haar-odd (equivalently `B`, via `13.6.2`), the conditional law of `y_n` given letters `0..n−1` is **exactly Haar-odd**: conditionally on the length-`n` cylinder, `y_0` is Haar on one class mod `2^{S_n+1}`, and the affine level shift (`14.15.1.4`) maps that class onto all of odd `Z_2` with unit slope `2·3^{M_n}`. Hence `(m_n, q_n)` is independent of the entire past, with `m_n` geometric(1/2) and `q_n` Haar-odd given `m_n` (the odd part of `y+1` at fixed `v_2` is Haar). Since `a_{n+1}` is a function of the past alone (part (iii)) and `3^{−a}` is an odd unit, the reconstructed window state at visit `n+1` has, under `B`, **exactly the product law**:

```text
omega-residue: omega_{n+1} mod 2^(k+2) = q_n * 3^(-a_{n+1})  uniform over odd residues,
               independent of the capped depth (and of the whole past);
depth:         d_{n+1} = m_n + a_{n+1}, with m_n geometric(1/2) INDEPENDENT of a_{n+1},
               and the law of a given exactly by 13.6.5.
```

This derives, rather than posits, `π_k`'s product shape — `ω`-residues Haar-uniform among valid (= odd) residues, independent of depth — and identifies its depth marginal as an explicit convolution. ∎ *(for all parts: the cited results carry the proofs; what is new is only their assembly, and part (iv)–(v)'s two short arguments above)*

**Theorem 13.6.4 (the genericity form of AEH).** Fix a **visit family**: a family of finite `F`-orbit segments indexed by a scale, together with a bulk cut — the bulk visits of one orbit's first `T` blocks as `T → ∞`, or the bulk segments of `13.2.1`'s uniformly sampled starts as `N → ∞`. Call a statistic's *bulk frequency* its limiting unweighted per-visit empirical frequency along that family, assumed to exist for every finite pattern; `13.2.1` is the assertion that the integers' ensemble family has the bulk frequencies of `π_k`. Say the family's letter word is **bulk-generic for `B`** if every finite letter pattern, at every fixed offset window around the visit index, has bulk frequency equal to its `B`-probability; say its window-state process is **bulk-equidistributed** if for every `k` and `L`, the `L`-blocks of consecutive depth-`k` window states (with their stratum labels, per `13.6.3`(iii)) have bulk frequencies given by the product law of `13.6.3`(v). Then, **for every visit family** — the dictionary below is deterministic and never inspects the family, so the equivalence holds in particular orbit by orbit, with no measure on starting values invoked:

```text
The letter word is bulk-generic for B
     <=>   the window-state process is bulk-equidistributed
           against the exact product law (13.6.3(v), depth marginal 13.6.5).
```

**Proof.** (⇐) Letters are *exact* functions of consecutive labeled window states: letter `n = (σ_n − s_n, s_{n+1})` (`13.6.3`(i); the labels are part of every depth-`k` window), so an `L`-letter pattern is a function of the `(L+1)`-block of window states, and its bulk frequency is the corresponding block frequency — which equidistribution sends to the `B`-value by `13.6.3`(v)'s law (whose letter-marginal is `B` by construction). (⇒) Fix `k, L`; for any `W`, the `L`-block of window states at visit `n` is an explicit function of the letter window `n−W..n+L+⌈(k+1)/2⌉` off an exceptional event of `B`-mass `≤ 2L(0.93)^W`, and both the function's level sets and the exceptional event are finite-window letter events (countably many; the geometric letter tails let every frequency be approximated by finitely many patterns). Bulk-genericity therefore sends each block frequency to within `2L(0.93)^W + ε` of the `13.6.3`(v) value for every `W`, hence to it exactly. ∎

**Corollary and recorded qualifiers (the honest scope).** The process-form equivalence above is at theorem grade; its identification with `13.2.1` as literally written carries one qualifier and one pointer, recorded exactly:

**Remark 13.6.6 (what the name buys, and what it does not).** By Theorem `13.6.2` and the Birkhoff theorem on a Bernoulli system, **Haar-almost-every odd `2`-adic point is `B`-generic** — its letter word has every pattern at exactly the `B`-frequency, unrestricted, no cut needed. The positive odd integers are a countable, hence **Haar-null**, subset; and for them the unrestricted statement is not merely unproved but false on every convergent orbit (absorption at the fixed point writes `(1,1)` forever — the bottom regime of `13.1`), so *some* restriction to the bulk is necessary. The bulk cut is not that restriction. Applied along a single orbit it supplies no infinite sample either: above a fixed cut a convergent orbit has finitely many qualifying visits and, once the cut passes its maximum, none. What makes the integer question nondegenerate is the ensemble — the sample refreshed at growing scale (`13.2.1`) — and within it the cut keeps the narrower job of excising the bottom regime. The genericity form therefore exhibits AEH as the assertion that **this particular null set inherits genericity at scale**: the bulk segments of uniformly sampled large starts have the letter frequencies of `B`, for horizons well past the digit budget at which that is provable. What the name buys: (a) it states the hypothesis's plausibility and its resistance in one sentence — measure theory proves genericity almost everywhere and says nothing about any prescribed null set; (b) it makes `13.3.3`'s exceptional tails the *non-generic points* of a Bernoulli shift — classical objects, the standard measure-zero residue of the ergodic theorem, rather than anything bespoke to Collatz (with one distinction to keep: infinite non-generic words are the right object for `Z_2` points and for hypothetical divergent orbits, whereas a convergent integer orbit contributes a *finite* word, of length growing with the sampling scale); (c) composed with itinerary.md `14.15.2`'s full-shift completeness, the two pages' facts form one picture: there is **no subshift constraint separating the integers from the generic set** — the letter language is free at every finite level, so whatever distinguishes integer orbits is invisible to finite-level symbolic structure and lives entirely in the genericity question. What it does not buy: any progress toward *proving* that a prescribed null set is generic — that is the hypothesis itself, not a reformulation gap; proof effort stays parked per the README stopping rules, and `13.3.3`'s scope stands unchanged (density statements about starting values at a finite horizon; individual staircase tails not excluded). At bridge.md `16.4.3`, the "exact missing hypothesis" bullet now has this as its symbolic name: the forward escape's missing statistical half is bulk `B`-genericity of the integers' letter words.

**Remark 13.6.7 (two equidistributions, not one).** Two different statements now carry the word "equidistribution" in this program's records, and they are distinct objects.
